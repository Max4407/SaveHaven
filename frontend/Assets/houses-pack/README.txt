HOUSES — pixel art asset pack
468 medieval buildings: 26 kinds, 3 variants each, in 6 conditions
(new, weathered, mossy, snowy, night with lit windows, abandoned).
Made with Pixel House Generator.

  Homes   cottage, hut, cabin, longhouse, farmhouse, townhouse, manor
  Trade   shop, tavern, smithy, bakery, windmill, stable, market stall
  Keep    chapel, watchtower, keep, gatehouse, tower
  Yard    well, barn, granary, woodshed, outhouse, dovecote, henhouse

  small/<kind>/   14 m = 96 px      a building and its shadow as two images of the same size
  large/<kind>/   14 m = 192 px     <kind>_<variant>_<condition>.png and ..._shadow.png
  manifest.json   every building: kind, group, variant, condition, walls, roof, file, pivot

ONE SCALE FOR THE WHOLE PACK
A well, a cottage and a keep stand together at their real sizes, seen from the usual
isometric angle. Barrels, carts and crates around a building are part of its image.

PIVOTS
manifest.json gives every image's pivot for both sizes: the pixel under the middle of the
building's footprint. Anchor the sprite there and the building sits on its tile.

SHADOWS
Draw the _shadow image under the building (or on a shadow layer at lower opacity). They are
the same size and line up exactly. Light comes from the upper left.
